CLUB HISTORY

Madamani Strikers FC was founded in 1987 in Kilifi County, Kenya, with a mission to develop local football talent and compete at the highest level in the region.


In 2025, the club made history by participating in its first-ever County League. Despite being first-time participants, the team put up a commendable performance and finished at 4th position in the standings — a remarkable achievement demonstrating the club's growing competitiveness.
